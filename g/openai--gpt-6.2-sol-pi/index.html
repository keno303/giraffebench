01 / Follow the feet

Watch from the side: a hind hoof lands, then the forehoof on the same side. The pattern repeats on the other side. It’s a four-beat walk, not a two-beat pace. Slow down or scrub the stride control to see when each hoof leaves the ground.

02 / Look closer

The two hair-covered horns are ossicones. The short mane traces the back of the neck, while narrow cream lines separate the chestnut patches of the coat.
