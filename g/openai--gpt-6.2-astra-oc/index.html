Field notes / 001

Reading the walk.

Giraffa reticulata

Watch the hooves on one side. The hind foot lifts first, followed closely by the forefoot on the same side. The opposite pair follows half a stride later.

The long neck rocks gently as weight shifts between the supporting limbs. At half speed, follow a single dark hoof through its planted stance and forward swing.

Identification

A network of pale lines separates the chestnut coat patches. The paired, skin-covered projections on the head are called ossicones.
