01 Field notes

Reading the walk

Watch the hind hoof on one side land just before the fore hoof on that same side. The other pair follows. That’s a lateral-sequence walk: four distinct beats, not two feet landing together.

  1. 1Near hind
  2. 2Near fore
  3. 3Far hind
  4. 4Far fore

Use quarter speed to see the foreleg fold and the hind hock flex. The planted hoof holds its place while the body moves over it. Switch to Portrait for the giraffe’s furred ossicones and the pale lines of its reticulated coat.
