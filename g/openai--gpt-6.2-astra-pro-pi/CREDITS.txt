FIELD ATLAS — PLATE 001: RETICULATED GIRAFFE
Image and software credits

The animal geometry, articulated walking system, real-time scene, acacia geometry,
interface and diagrams were created for this study. The giraffe is a moving 3D
surface, not a billboard. The photographic material studies below provide its coat.
